Before you start

To confirm you are at rehearsal, this page checks your phone's location once. Your location is sent once to confirm you are at the venue, then thrown away. It is never stored. Only "location confirmed" is recorded, and only for the rehearsal day. A record that you agreed is kept.

Step 1 of 3 · Who are you?

No QR needed. Sign in with the Google account you gave the choir, then check your location. Your phone is remembered, so next time it is quick.

To check in without the QR code you need to sign in with Google. If Google does not work for you, scan the QR code your Campus MD shows, or ask your Campus MD or Desk B to mark you.

The code from the QR you scanned is attached automatically.
