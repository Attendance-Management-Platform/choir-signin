Google Form day checklist

CCW Lagos Region RBC Choir. Use this when a rehearsal is taken on a Google Form instead of the QR code. Tick each box as you go.

Before the rehearsal (the System Administrator does steps 1 to 4)

The workbook is the backend, so the System Administrator sets up the form and its tab, so the data stays consistent and nobody edits the backend by hand. The Global Admin or Assistant tells the System Administrator the rehearsal date and asks for the form. Everyone else only uses the staff page.
  1. Copy the last form that worked.Do not add, remove or rename a question. The responses sheet must have exactly the same 10 columns and headings as before.
  2. Link the form to the workbook.In the form, open Responses, choose Link to Sheets, then Select existing spreadsheet, and pick "CCW Lagos Region RBC Choir - Check-in". Google adds a new tab of responses.
  3. Rename the new tab.Call it ATTENDANCE FOR dd/mm/yyyy with the rehearsal date, for example ATTENDANCE FOR 24/10/2026.
  4. Add one row in the REHEARSALS tab.Rehearsal ID: the next unused number, like R006. Date: the rehearsal date. Status: the same wording as the other rows, but not Cancelled. Attendance Sheet: the tab name, typed exactly as in the step above. Leave the other columns as they are.
  5. Check it.The Global Admin, the Assistant or the System Administrator opens Process on the staff page and presses "Check my Google Form days". Anything shown in red goes back to the System Administrator to fix.

After the rehearsal

  1. Wait for the day to appear on Process.It appears after 11 pm Lagos time on the rehearsal date.
  2. Enter excuses first.Use Enter an absence for a member for anyone who was excused.
  3. Process the day.Desk C, the Global Admin or the Assistant can do this.
  4. Answer every name that did not match.Choose existing member, new member or visitor, and give a short reason. Only the Global Admin or Assistant adds a new member.
  5. Correct one person if needed.Someone who was there but is not on the form, or was counted by mistake: Global Admin or Assistant, with a reason, before the day is published.
  6. Publish.Possible only when no names are waiting. After publishing, the day is final. A later mistake is recorded as a dated note on the day.
If the day does not appear: check the Rehearsal ID style, the date, that the Status is not Cancelled, and that the tab name matches the REHEARSALS row exactly.
