CCW Check-in: privacy notice

CCW Check-in is used by the CCW Lagos Region RBC Choir to record who comes to rehearsal. This page says what is kept, who can see it, and who to ask about it.

What is kept

  • Your member details: Member ID, name, email address, campus, part and membership status.
  • Your check-ins: which rehearsal, the time, and how you were marked (QR code or by a member of the admin team).
  • Absence requests you send, and the decisions made on them.
  • For choir staff: the role you have been given.

What is not kept

  • Your location. When you check in, your phone's position is compared once with the rehearsal venue. Only the yes or no result is used. The position itself is never saved.
  • Google sign-in details. If you sign in with Google, we only ask Google to confirm your name and email address. Nothing else is requested, and the sign-in token is not saved.
  • This website holds no member data. Your records are kept in the choir's own Google Sheets workbook.

Who can see it

Only the choir's admin team, and only what their role needs. For example, a Campus Music Director sees their own campus. You can see your own check-in and your own absence requests. Your information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone outside the choir's leadership.

Services used

  • Google: sign-in, email codes, and the Google Sheets workbook where records are kept.
  • Google Maps: the rehearsal venue address only, never your own location.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this public page, with no member data on it.

How long it is kept

Attendance records are kept for as long as they are needed to run the choir's attendance records.

Your choices

To see, correct or remove your details, ask your Campus Music Director or the Global Admin of the CCW Lagos Region RBC Choir.
